# Curvo Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-08-20

This Privacy Policy describes how ProjectorWarp ("we", "us" or "ProjectorWarp") collects, uses, discloses, and protects personal information in connection with the Curvo application ("Curvo" or the "App"). This Policy applies to personal information collected through Curvo, including the application binaries, installer packages, and related services distributed from this repository.

1. Purpose and Scope
- Purpose: To explain what personal information we collect, why we collect it, how we use it, and the choices you have regarding that information when you use Curvo.
- Scope: Applies to all users of Curvo and to all personal information obtained through the App and related support channels.

2. Controller
The data controller for the Curvo project is ProjectorWarp (Curvo Team). If you are an organization embedding Curvo into a larger product, you remain the controller for the personal data you collect from your users.

3. Information We Collect
- Account and Contact Information: name and email address when you sign up or contact support.
- Device and Usage Information: IP address, operating system and version, device identifiers, application logs and crash reports, and usage telemetry required for feature functionality and troubleshooting.
- Payment Information: If you purchase paid features through Curvo, payment is handled by a third‑party payment processor; we do not store full card numbers on our servers.
- Optional User Content: any preferences, configuration files, or files you choose to import/export within Curvo.

4. How We Collect Information
- Directly from you when you provide it (account creation, support requests, in-app forms).
- Automatically via the App (logs, telemetry, cookies where applicable for web components).
- From third‑party services you choose to integrate with (only with your authorization).

5. Purposes and Legal Basis for Processing
- Provide and maintain the App and features.
- Troubleshoot, diagnose, and fix bugs (including crash reports and logs).
- Improve user experience and perform analytics.
- Communicate with users about updates, security notices, and support requests.
- Comply with legal obligations.

6. Data Retention
- We retain personal information only as long as necessary to provide the App and fulfill the purposes described here, except where law requires longer retention (for example, transaction records).

7. Disclosure and Third Parties
- Service providers: We may share personal information with vendors who help deliver the App (hosting providers, analytics, crash-reporting services, payment processors). We require subprocessors to maintain appropriate security safeguards.
- Legal requirements: We may disclose information to comply with legal processes or to protect the rights, property, or safety of ProjectorWarp, its users, or the public.

8. International Transfers
- Curvo development and infrastructure may involve servers or subprocessors located in different countries. Where personal data is transferred internationally, we implement appropriate safeguards in accordance with applicable law.

9. Cookies and Tracking
- Curvo desktop installers and MSIX packages do not rely on browser cookies for core functionality. Components that use web technologies may use cookies or similar technologies for analytics or optional features. You can disable such tracking by adjusting the app settings where available.

10. Security
- We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information, including encryption in transit, access controls, and regular security reviews.

11. Your Rights
- Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal data, and to object to certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact us at the address below. We will respond within the timeframes required by applicable law.

12. Children
- Curvo is not intended for children under the applicable age threshold (typically 13 or 16 depending on jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have collected information from a child, contact us to request deletion.

13. Changes to This Policy
- We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the revised policy with an updated effective date in this repository and in the App where practical.
